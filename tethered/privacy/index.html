Tethered: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Tethered is a single-player puzzle game for Android (an iPhone and iPad version is planned). The short version: the game shows ads from Google AdMob unless you have Premium; it can send anonymous gameplay stats and crash reports to Google Firebase (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland only if you agree, and anyone can turn it off); it can save your progress to Google Play Games if you're signed in; and purchases go through Google Play. The developer never receives your name, email, payment details or anything you type.

Who is responsible

Tethered is made by an independent developer, reachable at tethered@muhammadkhan.dev. For the gameplay stats and crash reports below, the developer decides what is collected and Google processes it on the developer's behalf. For ads, purchases and Play Games, Google also processes data under its own Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you don't have Premium, the game shows an ad between levels now and then, and offers an optional ad to skip a level you're stuck on. Nothing in the game requires watching an ad. These ads come from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the AdMob SDK collects your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped). Google also uses this to prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to personalize ads.

Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form before personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Premium removes all ads, and with them AdMob's data collection for ads.

Gameplay stats and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To find levels that are too hard and to fix crashes, the game can send anonymous statistics to Google Firebase Analytics (when a level is started, finished or skipped, how long it took, how many tries and how many stars) and crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics (what the game was doing when it crashed, and device information such as model, Android version and free memory). Firebase also uses a random app instance ID and basic device information to count sessions. No name, account, email or anything you type is sent.

Your choice. If your device is set to a country in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks you the first time you open it whether to share these statistics and crash reports, and sends nothing unless you choose Share stats (the legal basis is your consent). Everywhere else, sharing is on by default (the legal basis is the developer's legitimate interest in fixing crashes and balancing levels). Wherever you are, you can turn it on or off at any time under Settings → Share anonymous stats; turning it off stops all collection from then on. The game decides whether to ask from your device's region setting, not your location.

See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Cloud save and achievements (Google Play Games)

If you're signed in to Google Play Games, the game saves a copy of your progress (levels unlocked, best times, deaths per level and your chosen look) to your Play Games account so you can continue on another device, and unlocks achievements. This data is stored by Google under your Play Games account, not by the developer. Play Games may show your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) in the achievement screens. You can sign out of Play Games, or delete its data, in the Play Games app or your Google Account settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

Premium, the Skin Pack and the gear are one-time purchases (there are no subscriptions), bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment and your payment details; the game never sees them. The game only receives a confirmation of which products you own, and keeps it on your device so your purchases work offline.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (levels unlocked, best times, deaths), your settings (volumes, vibration, control layout, your stats and privacy choices), your chosen looks and gear, and a record of what you've bought in the app's private storage on your device. It also keeps a cache of the sounds and music it generates so later launches are faster. None of this leaves your device except as described above. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it (purchases stay with your Google account and come back with Restore purchases).

Where data goes and how long it's kept

Data collected by the services above is processed by Google, including in the United States. Google transfers personal data from the EEA, UK and Switzerland under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Firebase Analytics data is kept for 2 months and Crashlytics crash reports for 90 days. AdMob, Google Play and Play Games keep data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and the UK), you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time (for stats: Settings → Share anonymous stats; for ads: Settings → Ad privacy). The developer can't link the anonymous stats or crash reports to you by name, but if you write in, we'll help however we can, including deleting Firebase data for your app instance. Reinstalling the game or clearing its data also gives it a new app instance ID. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Permissions

The game asks for vibration (haptic feedback during play; you can turn it off in Settings) and internet access (for ads, purchases, stats and cloud save). The ads SDK also declares the advertising ID permission and related Android ad-services permissions.

Children

Tethered is not directed at children under 13, and its ads are not set up for children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we'll help delete it.

Planned: iPhone and iPad version

An iOS version is planned after the Android launch. When it's released, it will work the same way, with Apple's services in place of Google's where they differ: purchases through Apple's In-App Purchase (Apple handles payment), and before any ad uses your device's advertising identifier, iOS will ask your permission (App Tracking Transparency); if you don't allow it, you'll still see ads, just not personalized ones. Stats and crash reports will follow the same rules as above. This policy will be updated with any differences before that version is released.

Planned: testing program

Before launch, people who join the game's test on Google Play can send feedback through a form. Anything you put in the form (for example an email address, if you want a reply) is used only to read and answer your feedback, and is deleted after the test ends.

Changes

If a future version handles data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
