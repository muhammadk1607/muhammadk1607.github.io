Tethered: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Tethered is a single-player puzzle game for Android. The short version: it collects nothing.

Information we collect

None. Tethered does not ask for, collect, transmit or sell any personal or device information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or other third-party SDKs, and it does not use the internet.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (which levels you've unlocked) and your settings (volumes, vibration, control layout) in the app's private storage on your device. It also keeps a cache of the sounds and music it generates so later launches are faster. None of this ever leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, used for haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

Tethered collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example cloud saves, purchases or crash reporting), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
